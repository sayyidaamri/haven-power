How it works

Haven is a vertically integrated distributed storage technology provider.

  • We design and manufacture purpose-built hardware.

    Our proprietary residential battery (19.2 kW / 41 kWh).

  • We deploy those systems efficiently at scale.

    Installed, maintained, and supported by Haven's in-house deployment team.

  • We deliver dispatchable capacity to utility partners.

    Fully dispatchable, local aggregations of batteries.

Utilities maintain full dispatch rights 24/7/365. Dispatch signals can be issued through the operator dashboard or via integration with your existing EMS or SCADA platform. When a signal is issued, Haven responds within seconds across the entire fleet.

Haven targets full deployment within months of program launch — not years. Our in-house deployment factory handles software, logistics and installation.
