Energy Advisor

Member Experience • Fort Worth, TX • Full time • On-site

Haven is building the distributed energy network that will power every home in America. We design, manufacture, install and operate home batteries that lower bills, protect families from outages, and strengthen the grid.

What you'll do

  • Own meaningful projects end to end, from first sketch to shipped result.
  • Work closely with engineering, operations and field teams to solve problems at the source.
  • Build systems and processes that scale as we grow from thousands to millions of homes.
  • Raise the bar for quality, speed and member experience.

What we're looking for

  • A track record of ownership and getting hard things done.
  • Clear communication and comfort with ambiguity.
  • Relevant experience for the energy advisor role, or a compelling story about why you'd excel.
  • Ability to work on-site in Fort Worth, TX.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and equity
  • Medical, dental and vision coverage
  • 401(k) match and paid time off
  • Daily lunch at HQ and a team that cares
