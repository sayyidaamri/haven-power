What to expect

Three steps, and we
handle the utility.

Get started
Step 1: Check your address.
Enter your zip code to see what Haven costs where you live. Your panels can be leased or owned. An existing home battery is the one thing that rules a home out, so tell us if you have one.
Step 2: Send a few photos.
Snap your electrical panel, meter and solar inverter. Our engineers design the configuration from there.
Step 3: Two visits, and you don't have to be home.
We prep the electrical first, then install Haven One. Your solar stays connected throughout.
