We've got answers

What Haven offers depends on your address. In Texas areas where you can choose your power company, Haven Energy + Backup pairs a home battery with electricity service, or you can pick Haven Energy for a fixed, low rate on its own. Where your utility stays the same, Haven offers the battery as a backup plan.

When your plan includes a 41 kWh Haven One, it supports the grid when demand is high — charging when power is cheap and sharing it back when the grid needs help. During an outage it automatically powers your home, usually before you notice anything happened. Enter your zip code to see what's available for you.

Energy plans start with a fixed, all-in rate per kWh that includes your utility's delivery charges. Backup plans add Haven One for a flat monthly fee with a refundable deposit — no equipment to buy and no install cost. Your exact price depends on your utility territory and appears on your Electricity Facts Label before you sign up.

Traditional backup means buying hardware outright. Haven owns, installs and maintains every battery, and earns revenue by helping balance the grid with our network of home batteries. That lets us keep both the battery fee and the energy rate low.

Yes. Members can share a personal link from the Haven app. When a friend gets their battery installed, you both receive a bill credit.

We buy wholesale energy and earn grid-services revenue by dispatching our fleet of home batteries at peak times. That's how we share savings with members instead of marking up the rate.
