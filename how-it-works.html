Power, with backup built in.
Here's how it works.

Haven is more than affordable power. A backup battery at your home protects your family from blackouts — and helps steady the grid everyone depends on.

Grid ONOFF

Haven works with the utility you already have.

In some areas Haven becomes your energy provider. In others — like co-op and municipal territories — your utility stays exactly as it is and Haven simply adds a battery to your home.

Same home. Same wiring. The power in your walls doesn't change.

The battery is what makes Haven, Haven.

It gives you seamless, automatic backup during outages and helps stabilize the grid when there's no outage.

Switches over within seconds · Whole-home protection

Your battery shifts with the grid.

It charges when energy is cheap — like overnight — and may share some power back when the grid is stressed. That's how we help keep the grid steady.

So if your charge level isn't always full, that's expected.

We always keep backup in reserve.

Your battery charge will fluctuate, but it rarely drops below the minimum we hold back for you. We watch the weather and forecast outages so your backup is ready.

We're always watching for shifting conditions.

Even on quiet nights our systems analyze the grid, the weather, and your battery's charge. When signs of trouble start to build, we adjust how your battery is managed so your home is prepared.

When the grid goes down, your battery takes over.

Backup kicks in within seconds. A single battery can run your home up to 36 hours — exact runtime depends on your usage. If your home briefly draws more than the battery can supply, the Haven app helps you bring things back online.

So, what happens next?

Step 1

See if Haven is a fit for your home.

Talk with one of our energy advisors, or take your time exploring if you're already in the right place.

When you're ready, enter your zip code to check eligibility and next steps.

Step 2

We'll review your setup.

Our engineers confirm your home qualifies. If it does, we'll tell you whether it supports one battery or two.

From there, we handle all the paperwork and logistics.

Step 3

Installation. We've got it covered.

Your install happens in two short visits. First we install the electrical equipment and get your home ready for the battery.

Within a few weeks we're back to install the battery beside your meter. Nobody needs to be home, and your power stays on.
