Got questions?

We've got answers

Answers on backup duration, warranty, pricing, and certifications.

Haven is an energy company building a better grid for the future, so families can enjoy affordable, reliable power. We design, build, install and operate home batteries that back up your power, save you money, and support the grid.

Haven One is our next-generation home battery, designed from the ground up to support the grid and keep your whole home running during outages.

A single Haven One stores 41 kWh. A typical home runs 12–18 hours on normal usage and up to 36 hours when you trim heavy loads. Two units, solar, or a portable generator can extend that to days.

There's no separate warranty to manage — Haven owns, monitors and maintains the battery for as long as you're a member. If something needs attention, we fix or replace it at no cost.

We use the battery to support the grid when it's under stress, which earns revenue that lowers what you pay. You get backup without buying the hardware.

Haven One is engineered to the UL and IEEE standards that govern residential storage and grid interconnection, and every unit is tested at our factory before it ships.
